How it works

From first call to a working bot

  1. Discover

    A short call about your task, your users, your documents and the decisions that must stay with a person.

  2. Set up

    We configure the bot on your data and rules, with the approval points you choose.

  3. Test

    Your team tries it on real cases. We fix what's wrong and show you how it was checked.

  4. Launch & support

    We hand over with a short guide and training for your staff, and agreed support.

What you can expect

Built for trust

Every bot we deliver follows the same rules.

  • Your data stays yours. We use only what you share, for your bot only.
  • Answers from your sources. The bot cites where an answer came from.
  • A person decides what matters. Refunds, emails and sensitive actions wait for approval.
  • Tested before launch. On your own examples, with results you can see.
  • Your team is trained. Staff learn to use the bot and to check its answers.
  • Clear scope and price. A fixed proposal before any work starts.
