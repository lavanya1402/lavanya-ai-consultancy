Our work · Teaching

Teaching & Learner Outcomes

How Lavanya teaches AI to learners without a technical background, and how we show what they actually learned.

Who she teaches

Business learners, educators and career changers

Lavanya teaches AI to undergraduate and postgraduate business learners in HR, Finance, Aviation, and Logistics and Supply Chain. She has also designed AI curricula, mentored professionals moving into AI, and supported learners on AI and data courses delivered on Coursera.

How she teaches

  • Clear explanation. Plain language before technical terms.
  • Subject relevance. Every example comes from the learner's own field.
  • Guided practice. Learners do the task in class, with help at hand.
  • Evidence and evaluation. Every output is checked, and learners say how.
  • Human responsibility. AI drafts; the learner decides and owns the result.
  • Documented deliverables. Work goes into a README and a GitHub page.

What we record

Evidence of learning, step by step

For each programme we keep the same record, so an institution can see progress from starting point to final work.

Starting point

The learner's background and level at the start.

Task and output

The task given and a sample of what was produced.

Documentation

The project README, screenshots and limitations.

Presentation

How the learner explained the work, and the rubric used.

Honest reporting

Five separate numbers, never one blended figure

Programme reports separate these measures. A GitHub link on record does not mean a completed portfolio, and we do not count it as one.

Learner names, roll numbers and marks never appear on this website. Examples are shared with institutions on request, with learners' permission and anonymised.
