What schools ask us

Common questions from school leaders

How do teachers start using AI tools?

With tasks they already do each week, so the first session saves time instead of adding work.

How do we check AI outputs for accuracy?

Teachers learn a short verification routine for facts, sources, difficulty level and bias.

Where does AI belong in student learning?

Right knowledge at the right age: foundations and imagination first, AI introduced gradually and with guardrails.

How does human review stay in place?

Every classroom material and assessment drafted with AI is reviewed and approved by a teacher before use.

Before you decide

What every school proposal covers

So there are no surprises on the day, each proposal states these points in writing.

  • Who the programme is for, and what prior knowledge is needed
  • Format, schedule and delivery mode (online or on campus)
  • What teachers will produce and show by the end
  • Which tools and accounts are used, and who provides them
  • What support is included, and where it ends
  • What we need from the school: rooms, devices, internet, a coordinator
  • A sample agenda
  • A relevant demonstration, such as teacher-reviewed question papers
