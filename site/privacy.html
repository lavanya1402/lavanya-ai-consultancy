Policy

Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information Lavanya AI Consultancy ("we") collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Questions go to lavanya.sriva@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive what you type: your name, email, institution, country, the type of enquiry, programme of interest, group size, timezone, your message and, if you give it, your phone number. We also record which page you sent it from and, if you arrived through a campaign link, that link's source. If you tick the updates box, we record that choice.

The AI Readiness Check runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal you asked for.
  • To send occasional programme updates, only if you ticked the box. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand which pages and events bring enquiries, so we can improve the website.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are saved in a Google Sheet in our Google account, and acknowledgement emails are sent through Google. Only Lavanya Srivastava has access. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless we are working together, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop sending updates, by emailing lavanya.sriva@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Cookies and similar technology

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores one small item in your browser for the length of your visit, to remember which campaign link you arrived from. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address to deliver them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
