For your approval process

What you receive before a programme starts

Institutional buyers need documents they can take to a committee. Every proposal includes:

  • Programme outline and learning outcomes
  • Delivery plan and session schedule
  • Assessment approach and rubric
  • Infrastructure and software requirements
  • Recommended cohort size
  • Reporting format: enrolled, attended, submitted, completed
  • How the programme is customised to your department

  3. Build
