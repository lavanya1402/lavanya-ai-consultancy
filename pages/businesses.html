Chatbot or AI employee?

A chatbot talks. An AI employee works.

A chatbot answers a question and stops. An AI employee gets what a new staff member gets on day one: an inbox, a phone number, a calendar and logins to your work software. Then it can actually do the job, and hand anything sensitive to you.

Your AI workforce

Six AI employees. One job each.

A bot that tries to do everything does nothing well. Each AI employee has a job title, a clear responsibility and a point where it stops and asks you. Choose the ones your business needs.

Front desk

AI Receptionist

Handles customer enquiries on WhatsApp, website chat, email or phone, and books, moves or cancels appointments in your calendar.

Asks you before: discounts, complaints, anything unusual.

Inbox

AI Email Assistant

Reads incoming email, sorts it by urgency and type, and drafts replies in your tone using your templates and policies.

Asks you before: sending anything new or sensitive.

Growth

AI Sales Assistant

Researches leads, prepares a short brief on each, drafts personalised follow-ups and books calls into your diary.

Asks you before: quoting prices or making offers.

Insight

AI Research Analyst

Tracks competitors, market news and customer reviews, and sends you a short weekly summary with sources.

Always: shows where each fact came from.

Back office

AI Operations Assistant

Prepares reports, quotations and invoices from your data, sends reminders, and keeps routine workflows moving.

Asks you before: any invoice or payment goes out.

Institutions

AI Education Assistant

Supports schools, colleges and universities with admissions enquiries, fee and class reminders, parent communication and paperwork.

Asks you before: anything about a student's record.
