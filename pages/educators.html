Modules

What the programme covers

Modules are selected and ordered to suit your institution and subjects.

  1. AI basics and limitations. What these tools are good at, and how they go wrong.
  2. Effective instructions and prompting. Getting useful drafts on the first try.
  3. Lesson and resource preparation. Worksheets, explanations and examples at the right level.
  4. Question papers and rubrics. Drafting, then reviewing for accuracy and difficulty.
  5. Research and source verification. Checking every claim before it reaches students.
  6. Data privacy and responsible classroom use. What never goes into an AI tool.
  7. Judging output quality. A repeatable review routine.
