What it does: agents study the official CBSE 2026-27 sample paper and syllabus, propose a blueprint and draft new questions with marking schemes. The teacher approves every question; only approved questions reach the paper and answer key.

Multi-agent customer support

Problem: support teams check orders, policies and refunds by hand, and automation without limits is risky.

What it does: a supervisor agent plans each request and delegates to order, policy and refund agents. Refunds above a set limit pause for a person to approve.

Secure workplace assistant

Problem: an assistant that can email anyone on anyone's behalf is a security risk.

What it does: an Azure AI agent schedules meetings and drafts emails through custom MCP tools. Each tool checks the user's role first, and a personal-data scan masks phone numbers, PAN, Aadhaar and card numbers, blocking high-risk emails.

Human Review & AI Evaluation

Testing AI before trusting it

Live demo

AI action safety benchmark

Problem: before an AI agent acts, someone must check the action against policy: allow it, ask for approval, or block it.

What it does: sends identical policy questions to two decision models and scores them on a held-out set of 60 cases, including the errors that matter most, such as allowing actions that needed approval.
