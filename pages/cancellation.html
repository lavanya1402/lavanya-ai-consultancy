Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: November 2026

No online payments at present

This website does not take payments. All programmes are arranged directly with each institution or client.

Institutional programmes

Fees, payment schedule, cancellation and rescheduling terms are set out in the written proposal for each programme, and agreed before any payment is made.

If we have to cancel

If we need to cancel a session, we will offer a new date at no extra cost. If no suitable date can be agreed, we will refund the fee paid for that session.
